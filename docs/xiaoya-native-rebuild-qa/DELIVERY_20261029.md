# 小芽 Apple Edition｜2026-10-29 UI reconstruction and verification

## Scope

Source: `little-works/xiaoya-apple/` in `ndshuge-galaxy`. This work is isolated on `xiaoya-native-rebuild-20261029`; the original XiaoYa and Studio editions are not changed.

Business domain remains unchanged: local tasks, schedule/CSV/ICS, growth goals, weekly review, AI provider consent, app-level approval and undo, private-data import/export, and mascot actions. Existing DOM IDs, persistence paths and business scripts are reused.

## Art direction

- Decision: hybrid Apple-inspired everyday planner. Primary question: "What should I do or schedule today?"
- Homepage: remove old stacked hero/tray, make day heading direct, give actions a compact green focus surface, place tasks and schedule into two independent content groups.
- Mobile: five primary tabs, **Settings remains accessible in top-right**; no 6-item compressed tab bar. Desktop: persistent labeled sidebar, readable content column and companion rail.
- Screens: grouped settings, contextual task form sheet, functional calendar, goals and reviews retain their working data surfaces.
- Materials: opaque data cards and one selectively glassy floating dock. No claim of native SwiftUI glass. The original project already executes `createGlass` in `apple-next.js`; this change imports the installed-source `createSpring` API from `glass/liquid-glass.js` in `native-rebuild.js`. Existing optics engine and license are preserved.
- Generic star symbol in `#i-spark` replaced by a sprout icon. Original branded XiaoYa artwork remains intact.
